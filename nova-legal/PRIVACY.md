# NOVA OS — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: [DATE]*

## The short version

**NOVA OS collects no personal data.** No account, no tracking, no telemetry, no analytics. What you do on your computer stays on your computer.

## What NOVA does not do

- It doesn't ask you to create an account.
- It doesn't send us usage data, crash reports, or "anonymous statistics".
- It doesn't show ads or build a profile of you.
- **ACE (Pro)** runs on your device. Your chats, screen, files and commands are not sent to us or to anyone else. ACE only sees your screen when you press **Allow once**, and only acts after you confirm.
- **NOVA Browser** blocks trackers by default. Its stats (like "trackers blocked") are counted on your device and never sent anywhere.

## When your computer talks to the internet

Some things need a connection. They go **directly to third parties, not to us**, and only when you use them:

- **Updates:** downloading packages connects to Arch Linux mirrors, which can see your IP address, like any download.
- **Websites and online services** you open (in NOVA Browser or any app) follow their own privacy policies.
- **Private web search** (if you turn it on) sends your search to the search provider you chose.
- **Tor** (if you turn it on) routes traffic through the Tor network.

We don't receive or store any of this.

## Our website

This policy covers NOVA OS itself. byeno.org may have its own privacy notice for things like a download page or a contact form.

## Changes

If this ever changes, we'll update this page and say so clearly in the release notes. NOVA will **never** start collecting data silently.
